ABOUT TEACHER RESOURCE HUB

Teaching should be about teaching — not struggling to prepare everything.

Teacher Resource Hub is a growing collection of teaching materials created to make lesson preparation easier for teachers in Ghana.

Resources are organised by class, subject, term and week so that teachers can quickly find what they need without going through complicated folders or websites.

The library is being expanded continuously with more lesson plans, lesson notes and schemes of learning.
