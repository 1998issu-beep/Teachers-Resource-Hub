ABOUT THE HUB

Making Teaching Resources Easier to Find

Teacher Resource Hub is designed to make quality teaching materials easier for Ghanaian teachers to access, organise and use.

From schemes of learning to weekly lesson plans and lesson notes, the goal is simple: help teachers spend less time searching and more time teaching.
